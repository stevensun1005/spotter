Spotter iPhone catalogue thumbnails are proportionally resized JPEGs of the web covers. Bundled Spotter MP4s are copied unchanged from the repository. The rig and mesh credits are in SPOTTER-3D-CREDITS.txt. If a bundle includes Flow or Workout Guide media, the source frames are resized and rasterized as timed GIFs. Workout Guide frames by Bryl Lim and credited Everkinetic artwork retain CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See WORKOUT-GUIDE-ATTRIBUTION.json for individual frame sources and credits. Regenerate the bundle with node tools/build-ios-catalogue.mjs.
